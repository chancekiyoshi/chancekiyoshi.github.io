Moving between Pohnpei and Hawaii makes you realize pretty quickly how different daily transportation is. In Hawaii, people have all kinds of public transit options like TheBus, trolleys, and the Skyline rail. But back home in Pohnpei, we don't have city buses or train systems. People rely on walking, driving their own cars, or catching a local taxi. Since not everyone back home can afford a car, taxis are a huge lifeline for getting around.

<div class="clearfix"></div>

## Identifying the Problem

Before starting this project, I caught myself thinking, "What can I build that would actually be useful for people back home?" Then it hit me: a taxi number list! Having quick access to taxi numbers is so important, especially for students living in the College of Micronesia dorms and workers at the FSM Capitol in Palikir who don't have cars. A lot of people work or go to school away from the main town of Kolonia and catch taxis back home every single day. Without a central list online, finding a driver's number when you're stranded can be a real pain.

## The Development Process

Working on this directory helped me sharpen my HTML skills. I had worked with HTML before, but it was a long time ago, so I was glad Claude AI could help me refresh my mind on structuring the page. Getting the actual data was a team effort, too. My best friend works at FSMTC and was able to provide the taxi numbers for the directory. Once I got the site working, I had my high school classmates back home test it out on their phones, and it worked great.

## Looking Ahead

I really enjoyed working on something practical, and I hope this directory ends up being super useful for the people of Pohnpei!
